Rotman Engineering Student Association

New Member FAQ

Welcome! The Rotman Engineering Student Association (RESA) connects engineering students with
industry, mentorship, and events throughout the academic year.

How do I join?

Membership is free for all enrolled engineering students. Sign up at the front desk or scan the QR code
at any RESA event. Your membership is active for the full academic year.

What committees can I join?

Events & Socials — plans mixers and the year-end gala.

Corporate Relations — manages sponsor and employer outreach.

Marketing — runs social media and design.

Who do I contact?

Email the membership lead at membership@resa.example.ca or drop by office hours in the Myhal
building, room 371, Wednesdays 2–4pm.
